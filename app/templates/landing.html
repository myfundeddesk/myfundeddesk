The Devil is in the Details.

We built this firm because we were tired of hidden rules designed to make traders fail. Here is exactly how we operate.

Drawdown Calculation

Most firms calculate daily drawdown based on floating equity, stopping you out unfairly. We calculate our 5% Daily Drawdown based on your Start of Day Balance OR Equity (whichever is higher). This protects you from intra-day massive swings.

Example: Start day with ₹1,00,000. Daily Loss Limit is ₹5,000. Your equity cannot drop below ₹95,000 at any point during that day.

Capital Scaling

If you achieve a 10% profit on your funded account over a 4-month period (with at least 2 payouts), we will increase your account size by 30%. This scales infinitely up to ₹1,00,00,000.

Scale Process: Automatic review every 4 months. No additional fees required to scale up.

Trading Styles Allowed

We welcome ALL trading styles. Scalping? Yes. Day Trading? Yes. Swing Trading? Yes. Holding positions over the weekend is fully permitted. Trading during high-impact news releases is fully permitted. We do not restrict your edge.

Expert Advisors & Algos

Automated trading is the future. EAs and custom algorithms are 100% allowed as long as they are your own strategy. High-Frequency Trading (HFT) and latency arbitrage are the only restrictions.
