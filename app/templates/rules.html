Trading Rules & Requirements

Strict, transparent, and fair trading rules designed to fund disciplined traders. Please read the rules carefully for your chosen evaluation model.

1. Trading Conditions

1.1 Weekend Trading Policy

Trading and holding positions over the weekend are not permitted. To limit exposure to weekend volatility, all open positions must be closed before the market closes on Friday (15:35 IST). Overnight positions are allowed only from Monday through Thursday.

1.2 News Trading

myfundeddesk allows trading during news events. Traders have full flexibility to open, manage, and close positions based on their strategy. Traders are expected to apply proper risk management.

1.3 No Maximum Time Limit

myfundeddesk does not impose any maximum time limit to complete the evaluation. Traders can take the time they need to follow their strategy.

2. Trading Rules & Requirements

2.1 Profitable Trading Requirement

Traders must complete at least 5 profitable trading days. A profitable day is where the account closes with a net profit of at least 0.1% of the account balance.

2.2 Profit Target Requirement

Traders must achieve a profit of 10% of the initial account balance to successfully complete the One-Step Evaluation.

2.3 Position Stacking Limit

Maximum of 3 trades per instrument in the same direction. A 4th trade is a soft breach. 3 soft breaches = Account permanently closed.

2.4 Trailing Daily Loss Limit

The trailing daily drawdown is breached if equity declines by more than 3% from the highest equity level reached during the day.

2.5 Maximum Trailing Loss Limit

Breached if equity declines by more than 6% from the highest equity level recorded since the account was created. Includes open and closed trades.

3. Funded Account Rules & Payouts

4.1 Profit Split

Traders on One-Step funded accounts are entitled to withdraw up to 80% of their total profits.

4.2 Withdrawal Cycle

7-day payout cycle. Must complete a minimum of 5 profitable trading days (>= 0.1% profit) within the payout cycle to be eligible.

4.3 Minimum Withdrawal

Traders must generate a minimum profit of 5% of the funded account balance before requesting a withdrawal.

4.4 Withdrawal Cap

Traders can withdraw up to 20% of the funded account balance during each payout cycle.

4.5 Consistency Score

Traders must maintain 40% profit consistency. The best day's profit cannot exceed 40% of the total profit requested for withdrawal.

4.6 Minimum Trade Duration

All trades must remain open for at least 60 seconds. < 60s is a soft violation. Max 10 soft violations allowed.

1. Trading Conditions

1.1 Weekend Trading Policy

Trading and holding positions over the weekend are not permitted. To limit exposure to weekend volatility, all open positions must be closed before the market closes on Friday (15:35 IST). Overnight positions are allowed only from Monday through Thursday.

1.2 News Trading

myfundeddesk allows trading during news events. Traders have full flexibility to open, manage, and close positions based on their strategy. Traders are expected to apply proper risk management.

2. Trading Rules & Requirements

2.1 Position Stacking Limit

Maximum of 3 trades per instrument in the same direction. A 4th trade is a soft breach. 3 soft breaches = Account permanently closed.

2.2 Trailing Daily Drawdown

The trailing daily drawdown is breached if equity declines by more than 3% from the highest equity level reached during the day.

2.3 Trailing Maximum Drawdown

Breached if equity declines by more than 5% from the highest equity level recorded since the account was created. Includes open and closed trades.

2.4 Floating Loss Limit

The total unrealized floating loss across all open positions must not exceed 1% of the account balance at any point in time. If breached, the account is permanently closed.

2.5 Stop Loss Requirement

Every trade must have a stop loss set within 1 minute of opening. Failing to set a stop loss within this window is a soft breach. Max 2 soft breaches allowed.

3. Funded Account Rules & Payouts

4.1 Profit Split

Traders on Instant Fund accounts are entitled to withdraw up to 80% of their total profits.

4.2 Withdrawal Cycle

Traders need 7 profit days (>= 0.25% profit) to be eligible for a withdrawal.

4.3 Minimum Withdrawal

Traders must generate a minimum profit of 5% of the funded account balance before requesting a withdrawal.

4.4 Withdrawal Cap

Traders can withdraw up to 20% of the funded account balance during each payout cycle.

4.5 Daily Profit Consistency

A single trading day's profit must not exceed 15% of the total profit achieved within a payout cycle.

4.6 Minimum Trade Duration

All trades must remain open for at least 60 seconds. < 60s is a soft violation. Max 7 soft violations allowed.

4. Disclaimers & General Policies

3.1 Inactivity Policy

If there is no trading activity for 21 consecutive days, the account will be considered inactive and permanently disabled. Traders must place at least one trade within every 21-day period to keep their account active.

5.1 Slippage & Market Conditions

During periods of high market volatility or major news events, slippage may occur. If slippage causes a loss that results in a rule breach or drawdown violation, it will be considered a valid violation and the account will be breached accordingly.
