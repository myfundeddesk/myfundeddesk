Trading Rules

Strictly enforced evaluation and funded account guidelines.

1. Trading Conditions

1.1 Weekend Trading Policy

Trading and holding positions over the weekend are not permitted. To limit exposure to weekend volatility, all open positions must be closed before the market closes on Friday. Overnight positions are allowed only from Monday through Thursday.

Example: Close all trades before the weekend start time on Friday at 15:35 and resume trading after the market reopens on Monday at 09:15.

1.2 News Trading

myfundeddesk allows trading during news events. Traders have full flexibility to open, manage, and close positions based on their strategy. Traders are expected to apply proper risk management and trade responsibly during volatile market conditions.

1.3 No Maximum Time Limit

myfundeddesk does not impose any maximum time limit to complete the evaluation. Traders can take the time they need to follow their strategy and trade with proper discipline. The goal is to demonstrate consistent performance, not to rush results.

2. Trading Rules & Requirements

2.1 Position Stacking Limit

Traders may open a maximum of 3 trades per instrument in the same direction. Opening a 4th trade in the same direction will be recorded as a soft breach. If a trader reaches 3 soft breaches, the account will be permanently closed.

2.2 Trailing Daily Drawdown

The trailing daily drawdown is breached if equity declines by more than 3% (or 4% on certain phases) from the highest equity level reached during the day, including unrealized profit and loss. If this limit is breached, the account will be permanently closed.

2.3 Maximum Trailing Drawdown

Maximum Trailing Loss Limit is breached if equity declines by more than 8% (or 5% depending on phase) from the highest equity level recorded since the account was created. This includes both open and closed trades.

2.4 Floating Loss Limit

The total unrealized floating loss across all open positions must not exceed 1% of the account balance at any point in time. If this limit is breached, the account will be permanently closed.

2.5 Stop Loss Requirement

Every trade must have a stop loss set within 1 minute of opening the position. Failing to set a stop loss within this window will be recorded as a soft breach. A maximum of 2 soft breaches is allowed.

2.6 Profit Target

One-Step Evaluation: 10% Profit Target. Two-Step Evaluation: Phase 1 is 10%, Phase 2 is 8%.

3. Funded Account Rules & Payouts

4.1 Profit Split

Traders on One-Step funded accounts are entitled to withdraw up to 80% of their total profits. Two-Step accounts can withdraw up to 90% of their total profits.

4.2 Withdrawal Cycle

Traders follow a 7-day payout cycle. To be eligible for a withdrawal, traders must complete a minimum of 5 profitable trading days within the payout cycle (a profit of at least 0.1%).

4.3 Minimum & Cap

Minimum Withdrawal: 5% of the funded account balance. Withdrawal Cap per cycle: 20% of the funded account balance. Any remaining profit stays in the account for future cycles.

4.5 Daily Profit Consistency

A single trading day's profit must not exceed 15% (or 40% consistency score) of the total profit achieved within a payout cycle. If exceeded, trading may continue until the total profit increases to balance it.

4.6 Minimum Trade Duration

All trades on funded accounts must remain open for at least 60 seconds. Closing a trade before 60 seconds is a soft violation (Max 7 or 10 violations depending on account).

4. Disclaimers & General Policies

3.1 Inactivity

If there is no trading activity for 21 consecutive days, the account will be considered inactive and permanently disabled. You must place at least one trade every 21 days.

5.1 Slippage & Market Conditions

During high volatility, slippage may occur. If slippage causes a loss that results in a rule breach, it is considered a valid violation and the account will be breached accordingly.
